Yesterday, I made my first Lithuanian cold beetroot soup at home, after having eaten it like 5 
times on vacation (in Kraków, then maybe again in Warszawa, in Suwałki, and definitely in Kaunas and 
Vilnius again).
I followed a simple [recipe](https://www.chefkoch.de/rezepte/168031072969651/Litauische-kalte-Rote-Bete-Suppe.html)
with simple ingredients.

The color indeed turned out amazing, I mean, just look at that bright pink!

![bright pink soup](/chlodnik.jpg)

Only downside was that I probably cut the beetroot a bit too coarsely, and that the Kefir used turned
the soup into a bit of a too watery state for my taste.
Maybe some missing śmietana?
No need to be sorry though, it was still very good.
Instead of small salad cucumber (what are those, do they ogórki kiszone?) I simply used the third-or-so
cucumber that I had left.

Just for the sake of completeness, in Lithuanian it's called "Lietuviška šalta sriuba".
